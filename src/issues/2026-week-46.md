    title: "MSCI is pushing the largest corporate Bitcoin holder out of its benchmarks under a rule that never mentions Bitcoin."
    what: >-
      Strategy leaves the MSCI USA Index, and with it the World and ACWI
      benchmarks, after the close on November 30, according to MSCI's November
      review published late on November 11. The trigger is a screen adopted
      October 15 that flags "non-operating" companies whose operating assets are
      under half of total assets and that fail four of five financial ratios;
      Metaplanet exits the MSCI Japan Small Cap Index in the same review. Tracking
      funds hold about 13 million Strategy shares, or 3.1% of basic shares
      outstanding, by the company's own count. The deletion neither delists the
      stock nor forces any sale of its 850,364 BTC. It does press on the premium
      Strategy uses to fund purchases: shares traded near 1.08 times the value of
      its bitcoin, down from 3.4 times in November 2024, and the company has said
      removal would have no meaningful impact on its business.
    why: >-
      Index eligibility is now a formal constraint on Bitcoin treasury companies,
      because MSCI's screen applies to any issuer whose balance sheet is dominated
      by non-operating assets.

  - category: Banking
    title: "Deutsche Bank switched on Bitcoin custody for paying clients, five years after it began building the platform."
    what: >-
      The service went live November 11 for corporate and institutional
      customers, booked through Deutsche Bank AG in Frankfurt with authorization
      from German regulator BaFin under the EU's MiCA rules and passported
      across Europe. It holds bitcoin, ether and three stablecoins — USDC, EURC
      and EURAU — with keys in hardware security modules, warm and cold storage,
      and multi-person approval for transfers. Asset managers, multinationals,
      wealth managers and sovereign institutions are on the target list; retail
      customers are not. The bank named no clients and disclosed neither client
      numbers nor fees. Custody, transfers and moves between storage tiers are
      the only services at launch, and assets held this way carry no deposit
      guarantee.
    why: >-
      European corporates and institutions can now hold Bitcoin inside an
      existing Deutsche Bank relationship, reached through the same Autobahn and
      Corporate Bank portals they use for other services.

    title: "The White House moved to refill two market regulators running on three commissioners between them."
    what: >-
      Four nominations sent to the Senate on November 12 pair a Republican and a
