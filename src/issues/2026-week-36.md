    title: "Sberbank activated Bitcoin-backed lending the day Russia's national digital-asset framework took legal effect — with Ethereum and USDT held pending central bank approval."
    summary: >-
      President Vladimir Putin signed Russia's digital-asset law on August 4,
      2026, effective September 1; the statute legalizes crypto assets as loan
      collateral and authorizes financial institutions to build regulated
      crypto-backed lending programs, while prohibiting digital assets from
      serving as payment for domestic goods or services. Sberbank — Russia's
      largest bank, projecting $46.4 billion in regulated crypto transaction
      volume in the framework's first year — announced on August 28, through
      deputy chairman Anatoly Popov speaking to TASS, that it would begin
      accepting Bitcoin as collateral on September 1. The bank had piloted the
      model in December 2025, issuing what it described as Russia's first
      crypto-backed corporate loan against Bitcoin held by AO Intelion Data,
      a mining operator. Ethereum and USDT are planned as additional collateral
      types, but require a separate Bank of Russia ruling permitting their public
      circulation — a ruling not issued as of September 1.
    consequence: >-
      Bitcoin's classification under Russia's new framework — as legally
      recognized loan collateral within a statutory lending structure, not as
      a payment instrument — establishes Sberbank's program as the first
      instance of a state-owned bank of its scale formally integrating Bitcoin
      into sovereign credit infrastructure under an explicit national law.

      On September 3, 2026, BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) drew $454
      million in net inflows — 62% of the session's $730.8 million total, the

      21Shares' ARKB contributed $138 million; Fidelity's FBTC added $74.45
      million, with the remainder distributed across six other issuers. The
      session sharply reversed a two-day lull: a $236.5 million net outflow on
      September 1 and $101.1 million of inflows on September 2 had preceded it.
      Combined net assets across all U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs reached $103.34
      billion at session end — approximately 6.32% of Bitcoin's total market
      capitalization.
    consequence: >-

      September 3 — more than all other U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs combined —
      establishing BlackRock's fund as the category's default destination each
