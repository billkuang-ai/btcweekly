      assume the regulatory obligations of a digital asset custodian.

  - category: Regulation
    title: "All Six GENIUS Act Agencies Missed the Stablecoin Rulemaking Deadline — Effective Date Moves to January 2027"
    summary: >-
      The one-year statutory deadline for the OCC, FDIC, NCUA, Treasury,
      FinCEN, and OFAC to publish final GENIUS Act implementing rules passed on
      July 18 with no agency having crossed the finish line. The GENIUS Act —
      signed July 18, 2025 — created the first federal framework for U.S.
      payment stablecoins, mandating 100% reserve backing in U.S. dollars or
      short-term Treasuries, monthly public reserve disclosures, and Bank
      Secrecy Act compliance. The OCC's proposed implementing rule set a $5
      million minimum capital floor. All six agencies remain at the
      proposed-rule stage. The statute contains no fallback provision — no
      automatic implementation, no interim guidance — so without final rules
      from all six, the law's 120-day accelerated effective date clock cannot
      start.
    consequence: >-
      The missed deadline triggers the GENIUS Act's eighteen-month backstop,
      setting January 18, 2027 as the effective date — extending regulatory
      uncertainty for the U.S. stablecoin market, which reached $315 billion
      in 2026.
