    title: "Strategy put conditions on selling Bitcoin at Q1 earnings — Saylor drew the floor days later"
    summary: >-
      On its May 5 earnings call, Strategy indicated it could sell Bitcoin if doing so was
      accretive to Bitcoin per share — a conditional framing delivered against a $12.54 billion
      first-quarter net loss, with 818,334 BTC on the balance sheet at an average cost of
      $75,537 per coin and roughly $1.5 billion in annual preferred stock obligations. In a
      subsequent interview, Saylor framed any sales as a small fraction of ongoing purchases —
      enough to remain a net accumulator every month — and noted that selling high-basis coins
      could unlock billions of dollars in embedded tax credits before proceeds are redeployed
      into new Bitcoin. The precise doctrine, he said, is that Strategy will "never be a net
      seller of Bitcoin," with purchases expected to outpace sales by a wide margin in every
      period going forward.
    consequence: >-
      The conditional framework preserves the accumulation thesis while adding flexibility:
      Bitcoin sales are a capital allocation tool for per-share value and tax efficiency,
      not a change in the underlying position.
