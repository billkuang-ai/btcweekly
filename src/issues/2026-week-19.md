    title: "Strategy put conditions on selling Bitcoin at Q1 earnings — Saylor drew the floor days later"
    summary: >-
      On its May 5 earnings call, Strategy indicated it could sell Bitcoin if doing so was
      accretive to common stockholders — a conditional framing delivered against a $12.5 billion
      first-quarter net loss, with 818,334 BTC on the balance sheet at an average cost of
      $75,527 per coin and roughly $1.5 billion in annual preferred stock obligations. MSTR
      shares fell more than 4% in after-hours trading. In a subsequent interview, Saylor
      clarified that any sales would represent a small fraction of ongoing purchases, and that
      selling high-basis coins could unlock billions of dollars in embedded tax credits before
      proceeds are redeployed into new Bitcoin. The floor, he said, is that Strategy will
      "never be a net seller of Bitcoin" — purchases are expected to outpace sales by a wide
      margin in every period going forward.
    consequence: >-
      The conditional framework preserves the accumulation thesis while adding flexibility:
      Bitcoin sales are a capital allocation tool for stockholder value and tax efficiency,
      not a change in the underlying position.
