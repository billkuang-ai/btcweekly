      market" — the first time he had publicly entertained selling any portion of its 818,334-BTC

      will "never be a net seller of Bitcoin" — expecting purchases to outpace any sales in
      every month and every quarter going forward.

      structured so that ongoing equity raises and Bitcoin purchases outpace any individual
      sales, leaving net Bitcoin per share on a permanent upward trajectory even if small
      liquidations are used to service preferred stock dividends.
