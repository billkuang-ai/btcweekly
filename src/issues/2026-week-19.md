      to unlock billions of dollars in embedded tax credits before redeploying the proceeds
      into new purchases. The precise doctrine, he said, is that Strategy will "never be a net
      seller of Bitcoin" — buying ten Bitcoin for every one sold, grinding holdings upward
      indefinitely.

      structure of STRC issuance is designed so that every Bitcoin sold to service obligations
      is replaced by ten purchased with fresh capital.
